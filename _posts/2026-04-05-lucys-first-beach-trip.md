Lucy had never seen the ocean before, and honestly, we weren't sure what to expect. Cocker Spaniels aren't exactly known for their love of open water — most of hers has come from a bathtub.

The first ten minutes were pure suspicion. She'd walk up to the edge, let a wave lap at her paws, and immediately retreat three feet like the ocean had personally offended her. Sand, on the other hand, she understood instantly — there was a lot of enthusiastic digging.

By the end of the afternoon she'd worked up the courage to wade in up to her belly, chase a few gulls she had no chance of catching, and roll around in the driest patch of sand she could find (right after getting wet, naturally). The drive home was one long, sandy, thoroughly satisfied nap.

## Takeaways for next time

- Bring twice as many towels as you think you need
- A long line leash gives her room to explore without a full gull chase
- Rinse the paws *before* she gets back in the car — we learned this the hard way

Would 10/10 recommend a beach day, as long as you're prepared to find sand in the house for a week afterward.
