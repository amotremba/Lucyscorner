On a bright October afternoon, Lucy took up her post in the sun, sat very straight, and looked at the yard as if it owed her an explanation.

Then the patrol began.

First came the perimeter, which she checked nose-down along the edge of the tall grass. Leaves were examined. Smells were logged. Somewhere in the middle of it, her head came up. Something had been noted. She did not say what.

After that she turned and walked straight toward the camera, in the manner of someone who has finished the paperwork and would like a word. The last stop was the wall by the basement window, where she stood and watched with great seriousness until the afternoon seemed to agree with her.

The yard, for the record, is secure.

## Lucy's official position

Yard secure. Pending further sniffing.
