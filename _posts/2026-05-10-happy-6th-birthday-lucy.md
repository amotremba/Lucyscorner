Six years old and still convinced every stranger on the street is there to say hello to her specifically. We celebrated with a homemade pup-cake (peanut butter and mashed banana, no sugar, very serious business), a new squeaky toy that survived exactly four minutes longer than expected, and — of course — far too many photos.

It's wild to think back to the shy, uncertain dog we adopted and compare her to the full-of-opinions neighborhood watch captain she is today. Six years of park trails, couch naps, and squirrel surveillance later, and she's somehow gotten even more herself.

## A few of her current favorite things

- The peanut butter pup-cake recipe (we'll share it if enough people ask)
- Absolutely destroying squeaky toys within the hour
- Supervising the yard from the highest vantage point available

Here's to another year of adventures, Lucy. 🐾
