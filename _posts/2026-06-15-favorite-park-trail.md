Three miles, two squirrels, and one very tired Spaniel. Here's how our Saturday unfolded.

We'd been meaning to try the wooded loop trail on the east side of the park for weeks, and Lucy made it clear within the first hundred yards that this was, without question, the best decision we'd made all month. Every log got sniffed. Every rustle in the underbrush got a full stop-and-stare. And somewhere around mile two, she spotted her first squirrel of the day and very nearly took me down with her.

The trail winds along a creek for most of the route, which meant multiple unscheduled wading breaks — Lucy's idea, not mine — and a lot of triumphant splashing before she'd trot back onto the path like nothing happened.

## Trail notes

- Shaded almost the entire way, great for warm afternoons
- Creek access at about the 1-mile and 2-mile marks (bring a towel)
- Squirrel population: high. Proceed with a firm grip on the leash.

By the time we got back to the car she was asleep before I'd even closed the door. New favorite trail: confirmed.
