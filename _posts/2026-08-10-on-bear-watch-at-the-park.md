Ever since the neighbor spotted a bear, Lucy's sniffer has been working overtime on every park trip — full alert, nose to the ground.

We're fairly certain the bear in question was a good half-mile away and never came near the park, but try telling Lucy that. Ever since word got around the neighborhood, every walk has come with an extra layer of vigilance: slower pace, more sniffing per square foot of trail, and a lot of pointed staring into the tree line.

Is it likely she'd actually encounter a bear on our usual loop? No. Has that stopped her from taking her self-appointed role as neighborhood watch captain extremely seriously? Also no. We appreciate the dedication, even if the threat level is, at most, theoretical.

## Current park protocol

- Extra sniff breaks at every suspicious rustle
- A noticeably slower pace through the wooded section
- Full alert status maintained until we're back at the car

Stay safe out there, Lucy. The squirrels remain the real menace.
