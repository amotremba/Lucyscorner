About Lucy

From a little timid to full of opinions

Lucy was adopted in July 2023 from a dog rescue. She came in a little timid and nervous, but that didn't last long — these days she's full of energy, opinions about squirrels, and strong feelings about anyone who knocks on the door. She celebrated her 6th birthday on January 1, 2026, with her favorite real dog bone.
