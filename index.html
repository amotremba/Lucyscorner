Meet Lucy

A good girl through
and through

Lucy is a 6-year-old Cocker Spaniel with floppy ears, endless energy, and a talent for stealing hearts (and socks). She lives with her human companion, who documents every tail wag, park adventure, and nap session for the world to enjoy.

This is Lucy's corner of the internet — a place to share her world, the products she loves, and the stories that make every dog day golden.
