# Lucy's Corner 🐾

A website dedicated to Lucy, a 6-year-old Cocker Spaniel and her human companion.

## About
Lucy is a Cocker Spaniel with floppy ears, endless energy, and a talent for stealing hearts (and socks). This site documents her adventures, favorite things, and everyday moments.

## What's on the site
- **Gallery** — photos of Lucy in action
- **Updates** — stories and milestones
- **Shop** — Lucy's favorite products
