## Notes on this draft

**No "I'm Anne."** That line belongs to `/p/lucys-corner` and stays there. An
About page that introduces the writer is really an intro post wearing the wrong
hat — and you'd have shipped two versions of the same introduction.

**"I write them. She supplies the material."** One line, still attributes the
writing, but no longer opens with your name. Reads as the publication's voice
rather than a person filling out a form.

**The list is set as a line, not bullets** — Cocker Spaniel. Fetch champion.
Professional napper. Squirrel watch. Doorbell alarm. Carries over from the
site's About section, which is the part that most sounds like a person wrote it.

**"Where this started"** does two jobs: gives the About page a reason to exist
beyond description, and gives your oldest post a reason to be read. That post
dates from July 31, when the publication had one subscriber.

**Disclosure stays in the body**, third paragraph. Mentioning affiliate links
plainly reads as honest and keeps people reading. Burying it works against you.

**"Most weeks" not a specific day.** Your story posts are irregular while shop
content runs every 2 days. A day promise would be a promise you can't keep. If
you settle on a rhythm, tighten it.
